7-day study plan

  • Day 1: Listen to your script two times. Read it aloud once. Learn 10 words.
  • Day 2: Vocabulary cards and a quiz. In Speaking Drill, get 80%+ on every sentence of your part.
  • Day 3: Full rehearsal in Full text mode. Check your time and speed.
  • Day 4: Full rehearsal in First letters mode. Practice 3 Q&A questions.
  • Day 5: Team rehearsal: practice the handovers between speakers. Use a real timer.
  • Day 6: Speak without the script. Do all Q&A questions.
  • Day 7: Light review only. Sleep well. You are ready!

Team checklist

  • Each person speaks for 4:30–5:30. Nobody is much longer than the others.
  • Each speaker ends with a clear handover: "Now I will give the floor to …"
  • Do not repeat the same example. Use the examples from the scripts or find your own.
  • Agree on who answers which Q&A questions.
  • Slides: few words, big pictures. Show real app screenshots.
